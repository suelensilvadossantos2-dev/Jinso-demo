# Jinso product knowledge for the demo advisor

Jinso is an AI-powered learning intelligence platform. This demonstration presents four confirmed concepts:

- Learners can take part in short, goal-focused practice conversations and receive concise feedback tied to demonstrated evidence.
- Teachers can review class-level learning indicators, identify learners who may merit attention, and receive suggestions for next teaching actions.
- Institutional leaders can compare engagement, progress, completion, and skill indicators across courses and reporting periods.
- Jinso can provide a conversational interface for exploring those experiences.

The classroom, learner, course, and institutional records in this demo are fictional and illustrative. The demo must not claim customer results, certifications, pricing, commercial terms, integrations, or production capabilities that are not listed here.
